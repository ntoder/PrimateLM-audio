# Examples

Real clips from the **test set** (audio the model never saw during training). Each card shows the audio, its spectrogram, the true label, and what the model predicted. We include mistakes on purpose — they show where the model struggles.

Cards are generated by `python model/make_examples.py`: for every class it takes the **first clip the model got right and the first it got wrong** from the test set. That's why half of them are mistakes. Confidence is the average over the clip's 2-second windows.

<small>Audio: Clink & Hamid Ahmad, [Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.14213067](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14213067), CC-BY-4.0. Clips shortened to 10 s.</small>
